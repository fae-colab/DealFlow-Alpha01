Schedule assumption: one agent, sequential non-preemptive actions; an action must finish by its deadline. The engine searches feasible precedence-respecting schedule prefixes exactly for portfolios of up to 20 actions. Multiple actions on one opportunity are allowed; positive commission lift is credited once per opportunity using the largest positive action lift, while negative prerequisite lift remains included. Expected incremental commission = net agent commission × (P(close with action) − P(close without action)). These are user-entered assumptions, not predictions.

CLAIM-LEVEL SUPPORT

Evidence & confidence

Record what supports each commission and probability claim. Confidence and evidence quality are separate.

Evidence before claims

Evidence quality describes the strength of support: none = no support recorded; low = weak, indirect or stale; medium = relevant and reasonably current but not independently corroborated; high = direct, current and independently corroborated or authoritative. A URL alone does not make a claim verified.

Confidence is subjective confidence in the estimate. It is not evidence quality and does not change the optimizer objective.

This is synthetic demo data. Its evidence must remain labeled synthetic with none quality; it cannot be promoted to a verified fact.

LEARNING LOOP

Record what actually happened

A recommendation is a hypothesis. Actual paid commission is the financial outcome.

Every submission appends a new event. Later submissions supersede the current event without deleting it; the full correction history remains visible. Leave actual commission blank when it is not yet known. A completed action followed by a sale is not proof that the action caused the sale.

EVIDENCE-LED RECONCILIATION

Compare recommendations with outcomes

Link a selected recommendation to its current outcome. Association is not causal attribution.

Actual paid commission and the standalone modeled action estimate are displayed separately. A standalone action estimate is not an allocated contribution to the total selected portfolio. The system will not calculate a predicted-versus-actual incremental difference unless a documented counterfactual method, evidence reference and reviewer are supplied.
